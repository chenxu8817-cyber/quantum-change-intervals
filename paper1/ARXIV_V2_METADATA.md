## Title

Quantum Change Intervals: Exact Asymptotic Localization with Collective
Measurements

## Authors

Xu Chen; Xue Ma

## Abstract for the arXiv web form

We study exact-label minimum-error localization of one transient change
interval in independent quantum outputs, allowing a collective measurement on
the full sequence. A known pure state $|\psi\rangle$ occupies one nonempty
contiguous interval and $|0\rangle$ occupies its complement. Let
$c=|\langle0|\psi\rangle|$, and let $p_1(x)$ denote the one-dimensional
Poisson-kernel Toeplitz functional. For fixed $c$ and fixed known interval
length $i$, the square-root measurement (SRM) and Bayes optimum have the same
exact asymptotic Toeplitz integral limit as the number of admissible
translations tends to infinity. If $i_n\to\infty$ and
$N_n=n-i_n+1\to\infty$, their common limit is $p_1(c^2)$. Under the prior
uniform over all $M_n=n(n+1)/2$ nonempty intervals, a triangular Følner
reduction and transfer to the non-Toeplitz physical Gram matrix give the
fixed-overlap limit $p_1(c)^2$. We also resolve moving overlaps $c_n\to1$.
With $\tau_n=n(1-c_n)(\log n)^2$ in a fixed compact interval,
$M_nP_X=[1+2\sqrt{\tau_n}/\pi+\sqrt{2}\tau_n/\pi^2]^2+
O(\log\log n/\log n)$ for the trace benchmark, SRM, and optimum. If $c_n<1$
eventually and $n p_1(c_n)\to\infty$, all three criteria satisfy
$P_X\sim p_1(c_n)^2$ without a monotonicity assumption. For a no-change
hypothesis with fixed prior $\pi_0$, a conditional localization limit
$\Lambda$ becomes the joint Bayes detection and exact-localization limit
$\pi_0+(1-\pi_0)\Lambda$; for fixed $c<1$, the weighted SRM attains this limit
for growing known length and unknown length.
