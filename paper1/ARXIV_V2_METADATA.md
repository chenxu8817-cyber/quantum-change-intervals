## Title

Quantum Change Intervals: Exact Asymptotic Localization with Collective
Measurements

## Authors

Xu Chen; Xue Ma

## Abstract for the arXiv web form

We study exact-label minimum-error localization of a transient calibrated
pure-state change occupying one nonempty contiguous interval in an otherwise
stationary sequence of independent outputs, allowing arbitrary collective
measurements. Let $c=|\langle 0|\psi\rangle|$. For each fixed known interval
length $i$, as the number $N$ of admissible translations tends to infinity,
the Bayes optimum and the square-root measurement (SRM) converge to an
explicit square-root integral of the corresponding one-dimensional Toeplitz
symbol. If $i_n\to\infty$ and $N_n=n-i_n+1\to\infty$, their common limit is
$p_1(c^2)$. Under the prior uniform over all $M_n=n(n+1)/2$ nonempty
intervals, the physical Gram matrix is not globally two-dimensional Toeplitz;
a triangular Følner reduction and an exceptional-sector transfer theorem give
the common limit $p_1(c)^2$. We also resolve moving overlaps $c_n\to1$. For
$\tau_n=n(1-c_n)(\log n)^2$ in a fixed compact interval,
$M_nP_X=[1+2\sqrt{\tau_n}/\pi+\sqrt{2}\tau_n/\pi^2]^2+
O(\log\log n/\log n)$ for the trace benchmark, SRM, and optimum. If $c_n<1$
eventually and $n p_1(c_n)\to\infty$, then all three criteria satisfy
$P_X\sim p_1(c_n)^2$, without a monotonicity assumption. Finally, for a
no-change hypothesis with fixed prior $\pi_0$, the joint Bayes limit is
$\pi_0+(1-\pi_0)\Lambda$; for fixed $c<1$, the weighted SRM attains this limit
for growing known and unknown interval lengths.
