\begin{abstract}
We study exact-label minimum-error localization of one transient change
interval in independent quantum outputs. A known pure state
\(\lvert\psi\rangle\) occupies a nonempty contiguous interval, while
\(\lvert0\rangle\) occupies its complement, and the receiver may apply a
collective measurement to the full sequence. Put
\(c=\lvert\langle0\vert\psi\rangle\rvert\), and let \(p_1(x)\) denote the
one-dimensional Poisson-kernel Toeplitz functional. For fixed \(c\) and a fixed
known length \(i\), under the prior uniform over the \(N=n-i+1\) admissible
translations, the square-root measurement (SRM) and Bayes optimum share an
exact asymptotic Toeplitz integral limit. If \(i=i_n\to\infty\) and
\(N_n=n-i_n+1\to\infty\), the limit for the same fixed \(c\) is
\(p_1(c^2)\). When the length is unknown and the prior is uniform over all
\(M_n=n(n+1)/2\) nonempty intervals, a triangular F{\o}lner reduction and
transfer to the physical Gram matrix give the common fixed-overlap limit
\(p_1(c)^2\) as \(n\to\infty\).

We also resolve moving overlaps near \(c_n=1\), where the exact-label baseline
is \(1/M_n\). With \(\tau_n=n(1-c_n)(\log n)^2\), uniformly for \(\tau_n\) in
each fixed compact interval, the trace benchmark, SRM, and optimum satisfy
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 M_nP_X&=
 \left(1+\frac{2\sqrt{\tau_n}}{\pi}
 +\frac{\sqrt2\,\tau_n}{\pi^2}\right)^2\\
 &\quad+O\!\left(\frac{\log\log n}{\log n}\right),
 \end{aligned}
\]
for \(X\in\{\mathrm{tr},\mathrm{SRM},\mathrm{opt}\}\). In the hull-dominant
regime
\[
 c_n\to1,\qquad c_n<1\ \text{eventually},\qquad
 n p_1(c_n)\to\infty,
\]
all three criteria satisfy \(P_X\sim p_1(c_n)^2\), without any monotonicity
assumption. For the fixed-overlap models treated here, adjoining a no-change
hypothesis with fixed prior \(\pi_0\), while
conditioning an anomaly on the same uniform priors, maps a conditional
localization limit \(\Lambda\) to the joint Bayes detection and exact
localization limit \(\pi_0+(1-\pi_0)\Lambda\). For fixed \(c<1\), the
prior-weighted SRM attains this limit when \(i_n,N_n\to\infty\), and in the
unknown-length model as \(n\to\infty\). At \(c=1\), the raw weighted SRM tends
to \(\pi_0^2\), whereas the optimum tends to \(\pi_0\);
maximum-a-posteriori relabeling restores the optimal limit.
\end{abstract}
