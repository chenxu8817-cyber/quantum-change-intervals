\begin{abstract}
We study exact-label minimum-error localization of a returning interval: a
known pure state \(\lvert\psi\rangle\) occupies a nonempty segment of an
independent \(\lvert0\rangle\) sequence measured by a collective POVM. Let
\(c=\lvert\langle0\vert\psi\rangle\rvert\), and let \(p_1(x)\) be the
one-dimensional Poisson-kernel Toeplitz functional. For fixed \(c\) and fixed
known length \(i\), the square-root measurement (SRM) and Bayes optimum under
the uniform prior over \(N=n-i+1\) translations converge as \(N\to\infty\) to
the corresponding Toeplitz-symbol integral. If \(i_n,N_n\to\infty\), where
\(N_n=n-i_n+1\), their common limit is \(p_1(c^2)\). For unknown length, the
prior uniform over all \(M_n=n(n+1)/2\) nonempty intervals gives the common
fixed-\(c\) limit \(p_1(c)^2\) as \(n\to\infty\).

For this unknown-length ensemble near \(c_n=1\), set \(L=\log n\),
\(\tau_n=n(1-c_n)L^2\), and
\(A(t)=1+2\sqrt t/\pi+\sqrt2\,t/\pi^2\). For \(X\) denoting the trace
benchmark, SRM, or optimum, uniformly over \(0\le\tau_n\le T\) for fixed
\(T<\infty\),
\[
 M_nP_X=A(\tau_n)^2
 +O_T\!\left(\frac{\log\log n}{\log n}\right).
\]
If \(c_n\to1\), \(c_n<1\) eventually, and \(n p_1(c_n)\to\infty\), then
\(P_X\sim p_1(c_n)^2\) without a monotonicity assumption. For the
fixed-overlap models, a fixed no-change prior \(\pi_0\) and the same conditional
anomaly priors map a conditional limit \(\Lambda\) to the joint Bayes detection
and exact localization limit \(\pi_0+(1-\pi_0)\Lambda\). For fixed \(c<1\), the prior-weighted SRM
attains this limit for a growing known length and for unknown length. At
\(c=1\), as the number of anomaly labels diverges, the raw weighted SRM and
optimum tend respectively to \(\pi_0^2\) and \(\pi_0\);
maximum-a-posteriori relabeling restores the optimal limit.
\end{abstract}
