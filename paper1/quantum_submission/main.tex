Exact Asymptotic Localization with Collective Measurements}

\begin{abstract}
We study exact-label minimum-error localization of a transient, calibrated
pure-state change occupying one nonempty contiguous interval in an otherwise
stationary sequence of independent outputs. Arbitrary collective measurements
are allowed. Let \(c=\lvert\langle0\vert\psi\rangle\rvert\) be fixed as the
sequence length grows. For each fixed known interval length \(i\), as the number
\(N\) of admissible translations tends to infinity, the corresponding Toeplitz
symbol yields an exact square-root integral for the asymptotic optimal success
probability; the square-root measurement (SRM) attains the same limit. If the
known length \(i_n\) and the number \(N_n=n-i_n+1\) of admissible translations
both diverge, with no restriction on their ratio, both the optimal success
probability and the SRM success probability converge to the one-dimensional
Toeplitz functional evaluated at the effective compound overlap \(c^2\), namely
\(p_1(c^2)\). Under a uniform prior over all nonempty intervals, the
unknown-length physical Gram kernel is not globally two-dimensional Toeplitz
because of gap-dependent corrections. A triangular Følner reduction and an
exceptional-sector Gram transfer theorem extend the comparison-kernel limits to
the optimal and SRM success probabilities of the full physical ensemble,
yielding \(p_1(c)^2\). After adding a no-change hypothesis with fixed prior
\(\pi_0\), while retaining the uniform conditional distribution over anomalous
intervals, the optimal joint Bayes limit is \(\pi_0+(1-\pi_0)L\), where \(L\)
denotes the corresponding conditional localization limit; the weighted SRM for
the augmented ensemble is not analyzed. Finite-size semidefinite programs and
dense physical-Gram SRM computations illustrate the asymptotic results.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} quantum change point; quantum state
discrimination; change interval; square-root measurement; Toeplitz operator;
Følner sequence; Bayesian detection
